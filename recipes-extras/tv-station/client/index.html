    An LLM invents a channel and writes its running order a few slots at a time. Each slot is generated as a short clip, and the clips are joined into one continuous stream; an interlude card loops whenever the next one is not ready yet. Clips are cached, so a restart resumes rather than re-renders. Nothing is generated until you press play.
